    #### Concept
    <div class="content">
        <div class="columns">
            <div>
                Vivid 2023 arrived at a pivotal cultural moment when people were beginning to come out of COVID lockdown and back into society.
                The festival's creative statement that year spoke directly to this:

                "...moving forward, to leave no one behind. To create moments of wonder that bring us together and shine the spotlight on what really matters — connection, authenticity, love, nature."

                The deeper insight came from observing our behaviour post-lockdown. After lockdown, coming back into society, we were all still strangers, but had all shared in the same experience.
                We formed a connection with people, without knowing them, but knowing we had a shared experience.
                This idea of individual people, threads, coming together through a collective experience, swirling around in this new society, sparked the idea for Sonder.
            </div>
            <div>
                Artists Michael, Joseph, and Brandon wanted to make something unapologetically Vivid, bold, full of light & colour, and undeniably cool. There was an intentionality here: a reminder of why people fell in love with the festival in the first place. They recognised a moment to reclaim that spectacle, that essential magic that made Vivid worth the crowds.

    #### Construction
    <DiagramTimeline>
        <DiagramLayer src="/assets/sonder/diagram/footings.png" heading="Footings">
            The baseplates serve as discrete anchoring systems for the light fixtures, integrating structural support with concealed electrical infrastructure.
            Engineered for durability, they needed to withstand the rigors of a festival environment, from accidental impacts from the thousands of people coming and going, climbing children, weather such as wind or rain, while
            maintaining absolute stability to ensure the light fixtures remain perfectly fixed throughout the installation's duration.

            The form was continuously refined to achieve a minimal profile and natural aesthetic, without compromising the structural integrity essential to the installation's performance.
        </DiagramLayer>

        <DiagramLayer src="/assets/sonder/diagram/connections.png" heading="Connections & Accessiblity">
            Positioned at the nexus of a major thoroughfare, the installation demanded careful spatial planning to maintain circulation flow.
            Universal accessibility guided the design, with minimum clearances thoughtfully calibrated to ensure all visitors, including users with accessibility requirements, could navigate and experience the artwork without impediment.
        </DiagramLayer>

        <DiagramLayer src="/assets/sonder/diagram/poles.png"  heading="Poles">
            The first 2 meters of the artwork were rigid LED tubes that were fixed to the base plates with a custom bracket system.
            Extensive testing with the team's engineers was conducted to ensure stability in a variety of conditions such as wind loads and user interactions.
        </DiagramLayer>

        <DiagramLayer src="/assets/sonder/diagram/artwork.png"  heading="Artwork">
            The artwork's infrastructure comprised an integrated network of LED threads, wired in concert to function as a unified system.
            This architecture was carefully considered by [automated](https://automated.net.au/projects/vivid-2023-sonder/), enabling the artists to animate the installation in its entirety or in isolated sections,
            offering expansive creative flexibility and real-time customisation of visual narrative.
        </DiagramLayer>

        <DiagramLayer src="/assets/sonder/diagram/mesh.png"  heading="Mesh">
            The mesh was the invisible hero of the artwork, suspending almost all the technical infrastructure above people's heads.
            Rigged by [Tri-Point](https://tripointrigging.com/project/vivid-sydney-2023/) and engineered by [Xavier Knight](https://xavierknight.com.au/vivid2023-sonder/)
            the steel grid worked to provide flexibility to the artists to adjust the lighting, while also providing enough structure to support the great wind loads while holding the lights and electrical equipment.
        </DiagramLayer>
    </DiagramTimeline>
